Why a website should unroll

Essay4 min read

Two seconds between arriving and reading is not a delay. It is a price tag: it says the thing inside is worth waiting for.

01Objects before information

Games know this. The best openings give you almost nothing to do and a lot to watch. One press of a button, and the world answers with weight, sound and time.

A website can borrow that rhythm without borrowing the graphics. The feeling of a game does not come from 3D. It comes from timing.

02The second visit

The first time, an opening is delightful. The second time you want to skip it. The fifth time it is a chore. So this scroll remembers you: after the first visit it is already open, and the intro waits behind a small replay button.

Ceremony once. Respect every time after.

03Slow is a choice

Nothing here is slow by accident. The paper eases, the ribbons swing, the ink takes a moment to settle. If you ask your system for less motion, all of it steps aside and the words are simply there.
